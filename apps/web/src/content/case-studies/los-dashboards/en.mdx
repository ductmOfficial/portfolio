## Context

Heureka Business Solutions, founded in 2014, builds AI-driven intralogistics optimisation and warehouse management software. Its main product is LOS, a logistics management web platform. I joined in October 2021 and have been its top contributor since: about 6,500 commits over five years and more than 40 modules for warehouse, transport and finance operations, built with React, Redux Toolkit, React Query and MUI.

## One platform, many sites

LOS runs for clients including Siemens (Nuremberg and Singapore), Loxxess, Bewital and ZooRoyal. I rolled out site instances in **Germany, Singapore, Mexico and Brazil**, each with its own KPIs, dashboards and warehouse layout.

## A widget framework instead of one-off dashboards

The core of that approach is a configurable widget and dashboard framework. Each widget gets:

- **per-user saved settings**, so each user keeps their own view of a dashboard;
- **date-range filters**, **aggregations** and **export**;
- **sanity checks** that flag implausible values.

The same framework powers the real-time, daily and analysis dashboards across all client sites.

## Drawing the warehouse

Dashboards need a floor plan to point at. I built a **Layout Builder** and a **Heatmap Builder** for warehouse floor plans, with area templates, aisle configuration and zoom/pan, and used them to deliver custom layouts for Siemens and Loxxess sites.

## Monitoring the operation

Beyond dashboards, I built the operational and business modules people work in every day:

- **Control Tower** shipment tracking with SLA timing;
- **Packing Monitor** and **Trucks on Site**;
- **Digital Billing & Price Sheets** and a **Ticketing System**;
- Leaflet map views, ApexCharts dashboards, and a full-screen **slideshow mode** for displays on the shop floor.

## Access and languages

Sign-in uses **Azure AD SSO (MSAL)**. Role-based permissions apply consistently to navigation, pages and individual actions. Translations come from the API and are rendered with i18next.
