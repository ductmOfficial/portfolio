## Why build it

Lumi (formerly CineScope) is my own project: a movie and series discovery app on the TMDB API, in Vietnamese and English. It is where I try ideas outside client work, with the same standards: typed end to end, tested, and checked in CI on every push. Live at [lumi.tmduc.workers.dev](https://lumi.tmduc.workers.dev).

The stack: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Query, Zustand, React Router, Tailwind CSS, and a Cloudflare Worker.

## What it does

- **A streaming-style home page.** A banner, a Top 10, rows by taste, by theme and by occasion (weekends, Tết and other holidays), and "continue watching" that jumps to the next episode.
- **Profiles for movies, series and people,** laid out like a social feed: a timeline, videos with a player that shrinks as you scroll, photos, cast, seasons and episodes, and where to watch.
- **Explore and search** with filters stored in the URL, followable themes, and related keywords.
- **A personal library:** watchlist, watched titles with ratings, custom lists shared by link, release reminders (notifications and an `.ics` file), and episode tracking.
- **Play:** a daily guess-the-movie game with streaks, badges, a year-in-review card, a side-by-side comparison of two titles, and "watch together", where a group swipes through options from a shared link.

## Personal without tracking

Recommendations are **rule-based, not AI or machine learning**, and every piece of user data stays in the browser. That made a few things simpler (no accounts, no database) and one thing harder: everything personal has to be computed on the device, quickly.

## Keeping the API token off the client

The app is served by a **Cloudflare Worker**, which also proxies requests to TMDB. The token lives on the Worker; the Vite config strips it from every production build, so it never reaches the browser.

## Quality gates

Every push and pull request runs lint (oxlint), type-check, unit tests (Vitest), a production build, and a check of the Worker bundle. The Worker's proxy and SEO logic have their own tests.

## Details I cared about

- Light, dark and automatic themes, plus a "Liquid Glass" theme that uses SVG refraction on Chromium browsers.
- Infinite scroll, and a layout that works from phone to desktop.
- Bilingual interface, Vietnamese and English.
